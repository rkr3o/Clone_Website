## ATS Technology Overview

### How ATS Systems Work (2025)

1. **Parsing**: Extracts structured data (name, contact info, experience, skills)
2. **Keyword Matching**: Compares resume content to job description keywords
3. **Scoring**: Assigns compatibility scores based on predefined criteria
4. **Ranking**: Orders candidates by relevance score
5. **Filtering**: Eliminates candidates who don't meet minimum requirements

### Modern ATS Capabilities

- **Natural Language Processing (NLP)**: Advanced systems can understand context and synonyms
- **AI Detection**: Some systems flag AI-generated content as potential spam
- **Plagiarism Detection**: Can identify copied content from job descriptions or other resumes
- **Machine Learning**: Continuously improve parsing and ranking algorithms

## Critical ATS Compliance Requirements (2025)

### File Format Requirements
✅ **MUST USE:**
- .docx (Microsoft Word document) - preferred
- .doc (older Word format) - acceptable
- .txt (plain text) - basic fallback

❌ **AVOID:**
- .pdf (unless explicitly requested)
- .jpg, .png (image formats)
- Any other formats

### Formatting Best Practices

✅ **DO:**
- Single-column layout, left-to-right reading flow
- Standard fonts: Arial, Calibri, Times New Roman, Georgia (11-12pt)
- Clear section headers: "Work Experience," "Education," "Skills"
- Standard bullet points (•) using Word's bullet feature
- Bold, italics, and color for emphasis only
- 0.75-1 inch margins minimum

❌ **DON'T:**
- Tables, text boxes, or multi-column layouts
- Headers and footers containing critical information
- Images, graphics, logos, or charts
- Fancy fonts or custom downloadable fonts
- Complex formatting or unusual layouts
- Hidden white text

### Content Requirements

✅ **MUST INCLUDE:**
- Contact information in document body (not header/footer)
- Job-specific keywords from the posting
- Quantified achievements with metrics
- Industry-standard terminology
- Relevant certifications and skills
- Clear, chronological work history

❌ **MUST AVOID:**
- Keyword stuffing or unnatural repetition
- AI-generated content (detectable by modern ATS)
- Copy-pasted job descriptions
- Irrelevant or generic content
- Abbreviations without full spellings

## ATS Optimization Strategy

### Keyword Research Process

1. **Job Description Analysis**
   - Extract 15-20 primary keywords
   - Identify required vs. preferred qualifications
   - Note specific tools, technologies, and certifications

2. **Strategic Placement**
   - **Resume Title**: Include target job title
   - **Summary**: 2-3 sentences with 5-7 key terms
   - **Skills Section**: 11-14 relevant skills
   - **Experience**: 8-10 action verbs with quantified results
   - **Certifications**: Industry-specific credentials

3. **Natural Integration**
   - Use keywords in context, not as lists
   - Vary phrasing while maintaining key terms
   - Include both abbreviations and full terms (e.g., "AI" and "Artificial Intelligence")

### Common Fatal Errors

1. **PDF Image-Based Resumes** (29% of failures)
2. **Missing Company-Specific Terminology** (24% of failures)
3. **Overused "Responsible For" Phrases** (18% of failures)
4. **Mismatched Job Titles** (15% of failures)
5. **Buried Keywords in Paragraphs** (14% of failures)
